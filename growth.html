How I work

Four steps, and I stay in it with you.

01

Get clear on your ideal customer

A short deep-dive into your business, your best customers and your market. Who you should be chasing, and who you should not.

02

A one-page growth plan

Where your customers are, the two or three channels that matter, and the one number we are moving. One page, not a slide deck.

03

Build the engine

Lead generation, a simple sales process, marketing that earns its keep. AI where it saves time or sharpens the work, and only there.

04

Stay in it with you

A weekly rhythm of focus, accountability and adjustment. You always know the next three things to do.
